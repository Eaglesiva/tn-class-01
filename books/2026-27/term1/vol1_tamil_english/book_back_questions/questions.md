## பகுதி 1: தமிழ் (Tamil Section) — Pages 1–84 (raw pages 11–94)

Structure: 4 units built around learning Tamil உயிர் (vowel) and மெய் (consonant) letters,
through "எழுத்து அறிவோம்" (letter-introduction) picture pages, songs ("பாப்பா" rhymes with QR
audio), and recurring activity prompts. The exact same activity-prompt types repeat for every
letter taught (அ, ஆ, இ, ஈ, உ, ஊ, எ ... க், ச், ட், த், ப், ற் ...). Representative verbatim prompts
captured below; identical prompt text recurs with a new set of letters/pictures on most pages
in this range.

### Unit — Letter recognition & writing drills (pages ~13–93)

Recurring verbatim activity instructions (each followed by picture/letter boxes, not reproducible
as text):

- "படத்திற்கு உரிய எழுத்தை இணைக்க." (Match the letter suited to the picture.)
- "எழுத்திற்கு உரிய படத்தை வரைக." (Draw the picture suited to the letter.)
- "எழுதுக." (Write.)
- "படங்களுக்கு உரிய எழுத்து எது? வண்ணமிட்டு எழுதுக." (Which letter suits the pictures? Colour and write.)
- "படத்திற்கு உரிய எழுத்தை எழுதுக." (Write the letter suited to the picture.)
- "க் - ன் கோடிட்டு இணைப்போம்; எழுத்தை எழுதுவோம்." (Let's join dots from க் to ன்; let's write the letter.)

### Unit-end summary ("முதல் பருவக் கற்றல் பயணத்தில்...") — page 84

Learning outcomes checklist stated in the book (verbatim):

- "படம் பார்த்துப் பேச முடியும்." (Can speak by looking at a picture.)
- "உயிர் எழுத்துகளை அடையாளம் காணவும் ஒலிக்கவும் முடியும்." (Can identify and sound vowel letters.)
- "மெய் எழுத்துகளை அடையாளம் காணவும் ஒலிக்கவும் முடியும்." (Can identify and sound consonant letters.)

### Fun with Letters (A–Z phonics unit, pages ~96–162)

Recurring verbatim instruction block that accompanies every 3-letter group (Aa/Bb/Cc,
Dd/Ee/Ff, ... Ww/Xx/Yy/Zz):

> "Say the letter.
> Say a word with the letter. (e.g. 't'- tiger; This is a tiger).
> Say the letter sound. Play the audio."

Each letter is paired with picture-vocabulary words shown on the page (not reproduced here
as they are picture labels, not questions).

### Learning Check — Worksheet, pages 129–130 (raw pages 139–140)

**5. Write the first letter of each picture and match.**
*(followed by pictures: lock, donkey, bag, fan, key, frog, bus, leaf, kite, dog)*

**6. Fill in the circles with correct letters.**
*(uppercase/lowercase letter-pairing grid: k–K, i, H, e, B, L)*

**7. Write the missing letters.**
*(alphabet-sequence fill-in: A, D, F, I, K, M shown; blanks for the rest)*

**Peer activity — Use This / That:**
> "Sit in a circle. Talk about the pictures in front of you. Use This / That."

### Learning Journey — Term 1 outcomes summary, page 165 (raw page 175)

- "Use the structures - This / That is a ___ and I can ____."
- "Name parts of the body; Talk about family members."
- "Greet others and introduce oneself."
- "Read sight words."
