### Lesson 8: Subtraction (Book pages 64–73)

**Let us Practise** (Book p.66, verbatim)
> **How many left?**
>
> | Starting group | What happened | Left |
> |---|---|---|
> | 7 roses | 3 fell down | ⬜ |
> | 3 pots | 1 broken | ⬜ |
> | 4 cats | 1 moved away | ⬜ |
> | 9 balloons | 3 flew away | ⬜ |
> | [balls on table] | taken away | ⬜ |
>
> *(Teacher note printed on page: "Familiarise children with real life situations where the words such as fell down, broken, moved away, flew away, taken away are used to denote subtraction.")*

**Let us Do** (Book p.67, verbatim)
> Arrange 9 tumblers. Throw a ball at them. Observe and answer.
> (Worked example shown: 9 tumblers, 3 fell down, 6 remaining.)

**Let us Practise** (Book p.67, verbatim)
> **Observe the pictures and find the answer.**
>
> 1. 3 flowers (2 crossed out) → 3 − 2 = ⬜ (worked example, answer shown as 1)
> 2. [4 footballs, 2 crossed out] → ⬜ − ⬜ = ⬜
> 3. [6 bittergourds, 3 crossed out] → ⬜ − ⬜ = ⬜
> 4. [5 pineapples, 1 crossed out] → ⬜ − ⬜ = ⬜

**Do and Learn** (Book p.98, verbatim)
