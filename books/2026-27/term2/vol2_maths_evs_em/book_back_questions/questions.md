## Part 2: Environmental Science (Term II)

Two units: **1. Our Green Friends** (plants) and **2. Genie's Garden** (vegetables/fruits).

### Unit 1: Our Green Friends (book pages ~68–93)

**Do and Learn** (p.74, plant parts)
1. One half of the tomato plant is missing. Draw and colour it. [labelled diagram: Flower, Leaf, Fruits, Stem, Root]
2. Some parts of the plant are not coloured. Colour and write their names.
   Word bank: Leaves, Stem, Flowers, Fruits, Roots

**Activity time — Flowers** (p.84)
- Why do flowers make us feel happy?
1. Make a Flower Bouquet.
   Madhu made a small flower bouquet and gave it to her Grandma.
   Now, make a bouquet using flowers and leaves around you and give it to someone you like.
2. Smell the flowers and guess their names!

### Unit 2: Genie's Garden (book pages ~94–112)

**Touch and Sort / Do and Learn** (p.94, vegetable textures)
3. Touch and Sort
   Let us play a game. I am going to draw four circles: soft, hard, smooth and rough. Touch and feel the vegetables and put them in the correct circles.
1. Tick (✓) the correct texture for each vegetable.
   Tomato: Rough / Smooth
   Yam: Rough and hard / Soft and smooth
   Bitter gourd: Rough / Smooth
   Bottle gourd: Soft / Hard

**[Fruits section]** (p.104)
2. Observe, compare and write the colour and size of the mangoes using the words given in word bank.
   Word bank: Colour: Red, Green, Yellow; Size: Big, Small
   [table rows: Nature / Colour / Size for Malgova, Senthoora, Banganapalli mango varieties]
3. Identify the seasonal fruits as they arrive in the market.
4. Project: Go to the market with your parents. Name the fruits and vegetables that you bought. Share it in class.

**Learning Journey** (p.112) — end-of-book self-reflection tree with 6 outcome statements (not questions, self-rating circles only):
- Identifies places where plants grow, their parts, their simple functions and demonstrates care and responsibility for plants.
- Identifies common flowers based on their observable features and uses.
- Recognises the importance of eating fresh fruits and vegetables and follows simple food hygiene practices.
- Identifies and compares plants and recognises that we get food from different plant parts.
- Identifies and classifies common vegetables, greens and fruits based on their observable features.
- Recognises seasonal fruits and different varieties of common fruits and vegetables.
