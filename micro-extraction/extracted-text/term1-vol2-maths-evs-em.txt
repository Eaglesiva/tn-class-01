Foreword
The early years of childhood are a time of wonder-when curiosity is boundless, imagination
is vivid, and every experience becomes a step in the journey of learning. These textbooks
for Classes 1 to 3 have been developed to honour this spirit, creating a learning experience
that is joyful, meaningful, and engaging.
Developed as part of the Tamil Nadu State Education Policy-2025, these books mark
a significant step towards strengthening foundational education in the State. The vision
and direction provided by the Hon'ble Minister for School Education have ensured that
the policy's emphasis on child-centred, competency-based, and inclusive learning is
meaningfully reflected in these materials.
The leadership of the Additional Chief Secretary, School Education has been
instrumental in ensuring the integration of 21st-century skills and knowledge, and in
shaping the textbooks into engaging story-driven learning experiences.
This is further reflected in a competency-based approach to curriculum and assessment
that moves beyond rote learning to foster deep conceptual understanding and meaningful
application. The integration of value education to build character, along with life skills, art
and motor skill development, enables the holistic development of the child.
The members of the High-Level Curriculum Expert Committee, the Chairperson
and the members of the Curriculum Development Committee, have provided constant
guidance, shaping and refining these materials through their insights and expertise,
alongside the contributions of educators and practitioners acknowledged elsewhere in
this book.
At this foundational stage, learning must build strong foundations while supporting
the overall development of the child. These textbooks bring together a carefully designed
set of features that make learning active, engaging, and meaningful:
•
 Opportunities for speaking and listening, building confidence in expression
•
 Activities that strengthen logical thinking and conceptual understanding
•
 Integrated spaces for reading and writing practice within lessons
•
 Fine and gross motor skill development through drawing, colouring, tracing,
and movement-based tasks
•
 Simple avenues for independent learning and self-reflection
The books also integrate values and life skills, helping children develop empathy,
cooperation, responsibility and confidence.
Learning is guided by a competency-based approach that moves beyond memorisation
towards understanding and application. Assessment is embedded within the learning
process, making it continuous, meaningful and supportive.
These textbooks are learning companions that invite children to explore, think and
express. They represent a shared commitment to enabling every child to learn with joy,
grow with confidence, and build a strong foundation for the future-advancing a vision of
"Education reimagined, inclusive, equitable, and future-ready".
DIRECTOR

Research and Training (SCERT)
Tamil Nadu

Distinctive features of the textbook
The Tamil Nadu State Education Policy (TNSEP), 2025 emphasises activity-based, play-oriented and
experiential learning at the primary stage to ensure that children attain competency based learning.
Assessment and Remediation
The activities suggested in the textbook develop logical thinking, analytical skills, mathematical
communication and 21st century skills through open-ended questions and exploration.
In the present technological era, the children get a lot of exposure through various platforms and
social interactions. Besides, the textbook is one of the resources for developing basic numeracy skills in the
children. Hence, teachers are expected to go for multiple resources for accessing to additional activities for
reinforcement of content, strengthening the content through multiple assessment tools for the effective
application of concepts in real life situations and for developing mathematical thinking among our children.
